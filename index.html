About Chat
World Chat
World Chat lets you talk with everyone online. You can mention online users with @. Messages support Edit Message, Delete Message and Reply Message through press-and-hold.
Private Chat
Private Chat is for one-to-one conversations. You can send text, images, video and voice when your browser/device supports recording. Messages show time and read status. Press and hold a message for Edit Message, Delete Message, React, Info Message and Reply Message.
Online Users
Online Users shows users who are currently online. Select a user and use Private to open a private chat.
Group Chat
You can create a group, search users and add members. Only group admins can add or remove members. Group messages support text, image, video, voice, reply, edit, delete and info. Group members can be mentioned with @.
Status
My Status lets you create Video Status, Image Status, Audio Status or Text Status. You can see who viewed or reacted to your status. Friends Status shows statuses from your private-chat friends. Other Users Status shows statuses from people outside your friends list.
Settings
In Private Chat, press and hold a private ID to open Settings. Settings contains Edit Profile, Notifications ON/OFF, Block, Unblock, Blocked Users, Remove ID, Logout and Back.
Notifications
Your Chat can show new private messages, group messages, reactions and status activity while the app is open. Notification sound can be enabled or disabled. Sound volume is controlled by the phone/browser system.
